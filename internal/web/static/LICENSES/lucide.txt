Lucide icons (ISC License)
==========================

Several glyphs in static/icons/sprite.svg are adapted from Lucide
(https://lucide.dev): house, book-open, layers, chart-column, user, volume-2,
rotate-ccw, x, check, arrow-right, arrow-left, keyboard, flag. The lamp, spark,
slower, play, theme, help, and tip glyphs are drawn for this app in the same
style (24px grid, 2px round stroke, currentColor). Path data is shortened to
one decimal place. Nothing is loaded from a CDN; the glyphs are inlined here.
