# Here is what I plan to do

## What I'll build

Brian asked on 2026-11-11 how to build a review that matches the research, after a sitting showed 3 of 157. One review lasts about 20 minutes, the block size in the one study we have, not 5 minutes and not a list long enough to feel like a whole day. Fast taps do not add more questions. Cards that do not fit stay due, and the next time he opens Review he gets another sitting of the same length. Today says about 20 minutes. A sitting already in progress that is longer than the new limit is dropped on the next load, so the 157 ends without waiting for a new day.

## What I'll reuse

The review already builds one queue from due cards and shows "3 / 157" from the length of that queue. The length today comes from filling about 25 minutes at his measured seconds per answer, which is how a fast pace became 157 questions. The 5-minute cap written this afternoon is a draft and will be replaced. Nothing was looked up outside this project. The plan checker was not on this machine, so this page was not run through it.

## How I'll check it works

A learner with hundreds of due cards and very fast past answers gets one sitting of about 20 minutes, and the question count stays far below 157. A learner with only a few cards due gets only those cards. After that sitting ends, opening Review again builds another sitting from what is still due. Today shows about 20 minutes. The check is a local test, not a production deploy.

## What I won't do

I will not open a separate project for this. It stays inside the lesson work already open. I will not change how cards come back on later days. I will not delete the learning record. I will not ship this to the live site.

## What you'll have at the end

Review opens as one short sitting of about 20 minutes. The counter cannot climb into the hundreds because you tap quickly. What does not fit waits for the next time you press Review.
