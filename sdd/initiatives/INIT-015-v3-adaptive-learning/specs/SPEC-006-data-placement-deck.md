## Description

**Context:** `internal/placement/items.json` holds five bands of 20 words, generated by `internal/placement/gen` and checked against Pealim (#85, `NOTICE`). Six items a band, with no word asked in both listening and reading, needs more than 20 words a band. Audio exists only where a clip matches the exact pointed text, so some listening cards are silent today. North-star sections 5.3 and 5.4 set the targets: 40 words a band, and no listening item without audio.

**Scope (in):**

- Grow `items.json` to 40 words in each of bands 1–5. Each word has pointed form, unpointed form, gloss, part of speech, gender (nouns), and band, and is verified against Pealim through `gen/curated.go`.
- Add six letter items for Part 0 (syllable audio, letter with vowel, three distractors).
- Generate audio for every new word with the existing `internal/audio` pipeline, and record the clip ids.
- A build test, `items_test.go`, fails when any word lacks an audio asset, a gloss, or a part of speech. It also fails when a band has fewer than 12 nouns or 12 non-nouns, so distractors can match part of speech.

**Scope (out):** Engine logic (SPEC-007). Unpointed forms are stored but not used (D4).
